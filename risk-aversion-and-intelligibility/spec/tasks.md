## Compare approaches on the same labeled cases

| Measure | Report |
|---|---|
| **Unsafe clearances** | `Expected SAME_ENTITY → DIFFERENT_ENTITY` and `Expected REVIEW → DIFFERENT_ENTITY` as separate counts, totals, and individual case audits. Show which cases became newly unsafe after a change. |
| **Correct clearances** | Count of expected `DIFFERENT_ENTITY` hits cleared. Also show the share of expected `DIFFERENT_ENTITY` hits cleared so abstaining everywhere has a visible cost. |
| **Clearance precision** | Correct clearances divided by all predicted clearances among labeled cases. Show numerator and denominator. If there are zero predicted clearances, report the metric as undefined. |
| **Other decision outcomes** | Full three-by-three confusion counts. Report match recall and predicted review rate with denominators. Report aggregate unlabeled review rate separately when useful for workload. |
| **Official decision cost** | Apply the organizer’s matrix on eligible labeled cases. Compare with the cost of reviewing them all. A local cost is a development result until checked by the official validator and provides no score on unseen cases. |
| **Evidence and repeatability** | With the validator, report accepted reasons and customer/source IDs. Inspect factual claims on changed clears and review flags. Compare repeated output and report runtime. |

Compare a structured baseline and each subsequent extraction or linking change on the identical inputs and fixtures. Require the positive-control clears and known unsafe-clearance fixtures to pass. Audit a newly unsafe clearance as a regression even if aggregate cost improves. When several versions satisfy the controls, compare how many additional safe clearances they earn and whether their review explanations improve. Where practice labels accept multiple codes or customer IDs, preserve the accepted alternatives in the scorer. The 50 practice labels support diagnosis. Treat sparse cases such as disagreement between plausible customer identities as gaps to cover with synthetic cases, and keep claims about unseen behavior provisional.
